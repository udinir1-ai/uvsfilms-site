It Is What It Is

2027 · 70 min · Gal Rosenbluth

In a time of war and division, my Israeli Palestinian love story is seen as taboo. When I uncover my grandparents' secret, I realize I'm not the first in my family who crossed the love lines. A story spanning three generations uncovers the hidden cost of mixed relationships in the “Jewish state”.

  • Director & Co-producer: Gal Rosenbluth
  • Producers: Udi Nir, Sagi Bornstein
  • Winner of the Tel Aviv-Yafo Award for Democracy and Freedom of Artistic Expression, DocLab TLV 2026
