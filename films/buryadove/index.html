Bury a Dove

2028 · 70 min · Noga Chevion

An 85-year-old Holocaust survivor dressed as Hitler roams the streets of Tel Aviv. This is Dov Or-Ner, whose life as an artist — burning and burying his art — was a symphony of creation and destruction. He is an iconic figure who dared to break taboos about Holocaust memory and Israeli identity. After his death, an immersive quest embarks to unearth his lost and controversial works, uncovering his tumultuous life.
